**Jake Dillon** is a 29-year-old Bucks County, PA–based singer-songwriter, producer, and music teacher. With nearly **1 million streams** and over **70 released songs**, he blends folk, country, and classic rock with heartfelt storytelling.

He has performed nationwide, including at Chip and Joanna Gaines’ Magnolia Silos, and has produced music for both himself and other artists. Alongside performing, Jake teaches music to **over 30 students**, sharing his passion for creativity and expression.

Recently, he has shifted from touring to performing locally, choosing to stay closer to family and community while bringing his music to weddings, events, and intimate concerts throughout Bucks County.
